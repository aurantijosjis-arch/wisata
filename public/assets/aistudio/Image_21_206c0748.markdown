## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a warm, grounded, and deeply human eco-tourism presence for a rural mountain village. The experience must evoke the feeling of stepping onto misty highland soil: genuine hospitality, clean mountain air, and rich agrarian tradition.

Key brand pillars:
- **Welcoming & Accessible:** Clear for multigenerational tourists (families, elders, independent backpackers) navigating on low-to-mid-range mobile devices under bright sunlight.
- **Organic Earthiness:** Rich foliage tones paired with warm sun-baked soil and cream backdrops. Avoid sterile, hyper-corporate minimalism or harsh stark whites.
- **Physical Warmth:** Soft tactile radii, pill badges mimicking hand-pressed botanical labels, and clear actionable touch surfaces that instill trust and immediacy.

The overall design style balances modern mobile-first utility with soft botanical tactility, prioritizing readability, high visual contrast, and effortless physical touch ergonomics.

## Colors
The palette derives directly from lush highland terrace farms, sunlit canopies, and volcanic soil:

- **Primary (`#1E4B38`):** Deep Forest Emerald. Anchor color for headers, dominant surfaces, main interactive boundaries, and institutional trust.
- **Secondary (`#4E9F3D`):** Fresh Leaf Green. Used for active badges, positive environmental indicators, progress states, and secondary actions.
- **Tertiary (`#D97736`):** Warm Terracotta Amber. Used for accent tags, price callouts, cultural experience highlights, and booking nudges.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1C2820`):** Dark Forest Slate. High-contrast, deeply readable copy tone ensuring WCAG AAA legibility against cream surfaces.
- **Neutral Light Canvas (`#F9F8F3`):** Soft Mountain Ivory/Cream base preventing screen glare outdoors. Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) is reserved for card interiors.
- **Utility Accent (`#25D366`):** Authentic WhatsApp Emerald. Strictly allocated to immediate chat reservations, guide coordination, and instant contact CTAs.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans is specified across all levels for its welcoming geometric structure, open counters, and high legibility at micro sizes on outdoor handheld displays.

Implementation guidance:
- Display and Headline tiers feature tighter tracking (-0.02em) with heavy weights (700-800) to maintain authority and warm presence.
- Body text uses generous line heights (1.5x minimum) and a minimum base size of 16px on mobile viewports to guarantee legibility for older visitors and casual travelers.
- Labels and interactive elements enforce semi-bold (600) weights to ensure effortless scanning across tour itineraries, room pricing, and facility listings.

## Layout & Spacing
A mobile-first fluid layout governs the interface, transitioning into structured container grids for wider screens.

- **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column fluid stack with strict 16px (`1rem`) outer canvas margins. Interactive items span edge-to-edge within safe margins. Gaps between related tour modules adhere to `space-md` (16px), while major narrative sections leverage `space-xl` (40px).
- **Tablet (768px–1024px):** 6-column fluid grid with 24px (`1.5rem`) margins and gutters. Cards arrange into 2-column paired grids.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column layout maxing out at 1200px container width with 64px (`4rem`) outer margins. Editorial side-by-side splits showcase village photography alongside practical itinerary schedules.
- **Rhythm & Touch Areas:** All clickable surfaces strictly respect a minimum 48px tap clearance on all mobile viewports to accommodate thumb interactions on the move.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth relies on soft, atmospheric light rather than synthetic dropped shadows:

- **Surface Tiers:** Baseline canvas uses `#F9F8F3`. Content modules and cards elevate via clean `#FFFFFF` containers bounded by an ultra-soft organic border (`rgba(30, 75, 56, 0.08)`).
- **Ambient Highland Shadows:** Floating elements (such as sticky bottom booking bars or modal sheets) cast a diffused, warm-tinted shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(28, 40, 32, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(28, 40, 32, 0.04)`.
- **Active & Pressed States:** Elements slightly de-elevate on touch with reduced spread to provide an organic, squishy, physical response.

## Shapes
A roundedness tier of 2 provides approachable, organic corners that complement the rural nature theme without feeling toy-like.

- Standard interactive controls, inputs, and list modules carry `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px).
- Content cards, photography containers, and detail banners carry `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px).
- Modal sheets, floating summary bottom drawers, and featured eco-lodge hero panels utilize `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px).
- Badges, status chips, and WhatsApp quick-action triggers employ fully rounded pill geometry (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to convey softness and friendliness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Bookings / Explore):** Solid `#1E4B38` with `#FFFFFF` text. Minimum height of 48px, horizontal padding `space-lg`, radius `0.5rem`. Subtle active depression feedback.
- **WhatsApp Direct CTA:** Solid `#25D366` background with pure white text, featuring an inline brand icon. Sticky to the mobile viewport base or full-width across package inspection screens.
- **Secondary Action:** Outlined in 1.5px solid `#1E4B38`, filled with transparent or `#F9F8F3`, text `#1E4B38`.
- **Tertiary Accent CTA:** Solid `#D97736` with `#FFFFFF` text for limited homestay slots or seasonal harvest festival tickets.

### Cards & Packaging Containers
- Card bodies sit on `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a 1px border of `rgba(30, 75, 56, 0.08)` and `rounded-lg` (16px) corners.
- Images inside cards are masked with matching upper border radii and bleed to the container edges.
- Card footers feature distinct meta groupings: price tags rendered in bold `#D97736`, distance/duration tags, and quick-action buttons.

### Chips & Badges
- **Eco Badges:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `0.25rem 0.75rem`, light leaf background (`rgba(78, 159, 61, 0.12)`), text `#2E6F40`, font style `label-sm`.
- **Harvest / Seasonal Tags:** Terracotta background (`rgba(217, 119, 54, 0.12)`), text `#D97736`.
- **Filter Chips:** Toggable chips in `#F9F8F3` with 1px border of `#1E4B38`, switching to solid `#1E4B38` with white text when selected.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF` with 1.5px border of `rgba(28, 40, 32, 0.2)`. Focus state shifts border to `#1E4B38` with a 3px soft outer ring of `rgba(30, 75, 56, 0.15)`.
- Minimum field height of 48px with generous internal padding (`space-md`). Placeholder text styled in subdued Slate (`rgba(28, 40, 32, 0.5)`).

### Lists & Information Modules
- Homestay amenity lists, activity itineraries, and village schedules use separated list items with hairline dividers (`rgba(30, 75, 56, 0.06)`).
- Leading icons enclosed in 32px circular leaf-green tinted badges (`rgba(78, 159, 61, 0.12)`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size 24x24px to guarantee effortless tactile selection.
- Border 2px solid `#1E4B38`. Active state fills `#1E4B38` with crisp white check/dot markers.
